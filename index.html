Our purpose

Building a legal profession that works with neurodivergent minds, not against them.

The Legal Neurodiversity Association is a membership organization for the people who make law work, and who happen to think, process, and communicate differently. Autistic, ADHD, dyslexic, and otherwise neurodivergent professionals have always been in this field. We exist to make sure they are recognized, supported, and able to do their best work.

We promote the common professional interests of neurodivergent people in law: attorneys, technologists, paralegals, operations leaders, and the firms and departments who employ them. That means education, community, and practical change in how legal workplaces are built.

Our work is grounded in a simple belief: neurodivergent talent is not a problem to accommodate at the margins. It is a source of insight, rigor, and originality the profession needs.

In short

Recognition, support, and belonging, made standard.

We connect neurodivergent legal professionals with each other, equip employers to do better, and give the whole field a shared voice for change.

The work we do

How we move the profession forward.

i.

Community & connection

A professional home for neurodivergent people in law, with peer networks, shared experience, and the simple relief of not being the only one in the room.

ii.

Education & awareness

Training, resources, and events that help legal teams understand neurodiversity and put genuinely useful practices into place.

iii.

Advocacy & voice

A collective, credible voice advancing the interests of neurodivergent legal professionals with firms, departments, and the wider industry.

iv.

Workplace change

Partnering with employers and sponsors to turn awareness into structure: better hiring, better accommodation, better careers.

v.

Partnerships & sponsors

Working with founding sponsors and partner organizations whose support makes this work possible and helps it reach further across the profession.

vi.

Member access

Member access to specialist coaching, consulting, and training through our partner network, connecting people with the right support.
